A quill, seems to be made from black swan feathers. The physical form outstand most of quill human ever made. It bears angelic beauty of black feathers, with the white lines ornaments on its edge. 
It size bigger than the most quill, similar from quill made from Kalhan Regions. While its origin where remain unfolds.
The quill were first founded during the fall of Eanstell Empire. It was founded by a knight in the ruins of Eanstell's Relic Storeroom.
It does not require inks to write. It has its own will to chose the word written. It can write on the air, does not necessarily needs a hard objects as surface. Each of lines written forms a luminous golden string-like effects, which would be highly visible even from miles away.
The quill were previously held by Johann Bernadotte, 'Marquess' of Galleant Kingdom before forcedly taken by The Watch Towers.
Legends said that many of quill holder use it to reform the reality and fates to follow their own will. For thousand of years the quill has been lost to unknown hands and has been a struggle for the world of livings.
Johann Bernadotte stated before his death during the seizure of the quill by the Watch Towers. "Those who wields it, would overcome the greatest power ever bestowed. With the consequences equal."
The most known phenomenon witnessing the quill power was during the reign of Termieme Ytternell. Where the holder of the quill openly, perform a wide-scale miracle. It is written that the impact of the miracle last for decades and remain unsolved. There's no written source of what miracle or what happened during. 
Citing from the report of Watch Towers. Quill Who Writes The World remain declassified or not categorized to fit the Relic scaling by the Watch Towers. It is might be unable to comprehend or it is beyond the scale capability.
